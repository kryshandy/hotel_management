# Fictional property media

These seven WebP images were generated with Codex's built-in image generation tool for the **Maison Wouri (Demo)** seed. They depict an invented property. They are not photographs of Hilton Yaoundé or any Cameroon hotel, and they must not be used to verify a real property's room features or accessibility.

| File | Final prompt subject |
| --- | --- |
| `courtyard-hero.webp` | Wide, serene fictional Douala hotel courtyard, tropical greenery, reflecting pool, warm timber screens, and dark space at left for headline copy. |
| `classic-king.webp` | Contemporary king room with white linen, dark timber, woven textile detail, and natural light. |
| `classic-twin.webp` | Two separate twin beds in a calm contemporary room with natural light. |
| `deluxe-balcony.webp` | King room with a sitting chair and a balcony overlooking tropical city greenery. |
| `family-room.webp` | Family room with a king bed, a daybed, warm wood, and a compact seating corner. |
| `accessible-queen.webp` | Queen room concept with wide circulation and unobstructed doorway; actual access features must be confirmed by a real operator. |
| `executive-suite.webp` | King suite with a distinct lounge area, warm wood, neutral woven fabrics, and subtle blue accents. |

All prompts requested horizontal, photorealistic editorial hotel photography with no people, text, logos, watermarks, or real-property branding. The generated PNGs were converted to WebP at quality 84 for the website. The seed references these checked-in files under `/demo-media/`.
